## zh-Hans

Name: 我在旅途-梦游
Subtitle: 发现下一次值得去的旅程
Promotional text: 不从热门榜单出发，而从你想经历什么开始。背包、自驾或旅居，探索季节、天数与体验恰好合适的下一程。
Keywords: 旅行灵感,背包旅行,自驾游,旅居,目的地推荐,旅行规划,季节旅行

Description:
下一次旅行，去哪儿才值得？

我在旅途-梦游把推荐单位从单个目的地变成完整的旅行体验：去哪里、什么时候去、停留多久、最值得经历什么。首页分别呈现背包、自驾和旅居三种灵感，让你先看方向，再决定是否深入研究。

你可以用自然语言说出自己的时间、出发地、天数、同行人、天气偏好或体力要求；也可以输入已经确定的旅程，让应用围绕准备事项继续思考。快速、中度、深度三档研究方式，让探索时间与投入由你决定。

推荐会尽量说明吸引力，也说明到达成本、季节限制和需要接受的取舍。没有配置 AI 时，仍可查看明确标注的示例灵感卡片。

主要功能：
• 背包、自驾、旅居三类旅行灵感
• 自然语言描述旅行愿望或已确定计划
• 三档研究深度与可停止的研究过程
• 对季节、停留时间和体验重点的综合呈现
• 简体中文与英文界面随 iPhone 语言切换

使用方法：先浏览示例卡片；如需新的 AI 研究，请在设置中选择 OpenAI、Claude、Gemini、DeepSeek、通义千问、Kimi、智谱、豆包或文心，填写自己的 API Key，阅读数据共享说明并同意，然后选研究深度并开始。部分研究可能需要较长时间，也可能产生服务商 API 费用。

请注意：AI 旅行研究不是预订服务，也不能保证地点、路线、天气、安全、签证或价格信息准确或实时。出行前请核查官方资料与供应商条款。示例卡片为演示内容，不代表已完成实时核实。

## en-US

Name: My Journey Dream
Subtitle: Find your next great journey
Promotional text: Go beyond destination lists. Discover backpack journeys, road trips, and slow stays matched to season, duration, and the experiences you want.
Keywords: travel inspiration,backpack travel,road trips,slow travel,trip ideas,seasonal travel

Description:
Where should your next journey take you—and why?

My Journey Dream thinks in complete experiences, not just destination names: where to go, when to go, how long to stay, and what makes the trip worthwhile. Start with three different kinds of inspiration—backpack journeys, road trips, and slow stays—then explore the one that speaks to you.

Describe your available dates, departure city, travel time, companions, weather preferences, or physical comfort in everyday language. Already know where you are going? Enter the confirmed plan and use the app to think through preparation. Choose quick, balanced, or deep research to control how much time you spend exploring.

Recommendations aim to show both the appeal and the tradeoffs: seasonality, access, effort, and things you may need to accept. Clearly labeled sample cards are available without connecting an AI provider.

Highlights:
• Backpack, road trip, and slow-stay inspiration
• Natural-language requests for new or confirmed journeys
• Three research depths and a stop control
• Season, duration, and experience context
• English and Simplified Chinese interfaces that follow your iPhone language

Getting started: Browse the sample ideas. For fresh AI research, choose OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi, GLM, Doubao, or ERNIE in Settings, enter your own API key, review and accept the data-sharing disclosure, choose a research depth, and start. Research can take time and may incur API charges from your provider.

Important: AI research is not a booking service and cannot guarantee that routes, weather, safety, entry rules, prices, or availability are accurate or current. Check official sources and supplier terms before committing to a trip. Sample cards are illustrative, not live-verified recommendations.
